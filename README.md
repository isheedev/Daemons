🩸 Daemons — A Retro Boomer Shooter Built in Unity

Daemons is a fast, furious, and unapologetically old-school boomer shooter forged in Unity with C#. Inspired by the carnage of DOOM, Quake, and Blood, it delivers high-speed gunplay, gory feedback, and a relentless atmosphere of demonic chaos.

⚔️ Core Features

Classic movement, modern feel – Bunny-hop, strafe, and blast your way through infernal arenas with buttery-smooth controls and responsive physics.

Devastating arsenal – Wield iconic weapons that hit hard, sound mean, and paint the walls red.

Stylized retro visuals – Chunky pixels, dynamic lighting, and gritty shaders that bridge 90s nostalgia with modern rendering.

Smart AI & combat design – Enemies that swarm, flank, and punish hesitation.

Heavy atmosphere – Pulse-pounding metal soundtrack and sound design that makes every encounter visceral.

Daemons aims to channel the spirit of 90s FPS classics — raw speed, brutal combat, and total immersion — rebuilt with modern Unity tech. Every mechanic, visual, and sound is designed to deliver the adrenaline rush that defined an era.
